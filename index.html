Selected research

Aug 2026 — Present

Double-Latch Switching in Divided-Body SOI Transistors

KAIST · NOBELab · Silvaco ATLAS

I use Silvaco ATLAS to study how two separate latch transitions can arise in a divided-body SOI transistor.

  • Built an SOI structure with two body regions separated by an n+ island and a resistive tap.
  • Observed latch transitions at 2.254 V and 3.296 V in drift-diffusion simulations.
  • Tested how doping, island geometry, carrier lifetime, and tap resistance affect the hysteresis.

Feb — Jun 2025

Modeling Human Vestibular Encoding on a Neuromorphic Chip

ETH Zürich · Institute of Neuroinformatics · Prof. Giacomo Indiveri / Prof. Elisa Donati

At ETH's Institute of Neuroinformatics, I used DYNAP-SE1 to study how vestibular signals are represented by spiking neurons.

  • Applied sinusoidal current directly to the chip instead of using an FPGA-generated input spike train.
  • Analyzed firing rates, inter-spike intervals, CV, Welch PSD, coherence, and gain.

Jan — May 2026

LLM Emotion Control by Vector Steering

KAIST · Individual Research · Prof. Sang-Wan Lee

I studied whether one language model could influence another model's emotional responses during a multi-turn conversation.

  • Applied hidden-state activation steering to selected transformer layers.
  • Used emotion classifiers and Bayesian optimization to tune the controller and compare it with prompt-based interventions.

Jun — Dec 2024

Alpha-Synuclein Detection Using Brain Organoids

KAIST · Individual Research · Prof. Minee Choi

I worked on circular dichroism measurements of α-synuclein in midbrain organoid-conditioned media.

  • Optimized measurements using recombinant α-synuclein controls.
  • Compared conditions including concentration, dilution, and optical path length.
