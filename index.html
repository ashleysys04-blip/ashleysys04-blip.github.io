01 / Research vision

Computation that emerges from physics.

My research spans sensory encoding on mixed-signal neuromorphic chips, nonlinear silicon devices, and brain-inspired learning. I hope to bridge device physics, circuits, and neural computation to build systems in which physical dynamics perform meaningful computation.

“How can we move computation from separate digital blocks into the intrinsic dynamics of devices and circuits?”

01 / DEVICE PHYSICS / NEUROMORPHIC HARDWAREJun 2026 — Present

Hysteretic Push–Pull Silicon Devices for History-Dependent Vestibular Encoding

KAIST · NOBELab · Prof. Yang-Kyu Choi

Investigating how latch transitions and hysteresis in silicon devices can represent input history and support physically implemented sensory-state encoding.

  • Built Silvaco ATLAS TCAD models for MOSFET, SOI, and SONOS device structures.
  • Identified and studied double-latch behavior in SOI transistors, exploring threshold and hysteresis-window tuning.
  • Exploring multi-state device behavior and possible roles in excitatory/inhibitory neuromorphic circuits.

02 / NEUROMORPHIC SYSTEMS / NEURAL ENCODINGFeb — Jun 2025

Modeling Human Vestibular Encoding on a Neuromorphic Chip

ETH Zürich · Institute of Neuroinformatics · Prof. Giacomo Indiveri / Elisa Donati

Modeled vestibular afferent encoding using the DYNAP-SE1 mixed-signal spiking processor, translating sinusoidal sensory stimuli into population spike responses.

  • Injected sinusoidal current stimuli directly into neuromorphic neuron circuits.
  • Analyzed inter-spike intervals, firing variability, Welch power spectral density, coherence, and gain.
  • Studied the fidelity of frequency encoding and the role of network dynamics in irregular spiking.

03 / AI / BRAIN-INSPIRED LEARNINGJan — May 2026

LLM Emotion Control by Vector Steering

KAIST · Individual Research · Prof. Sang-Wan Lee

Developed a two-agent dialogue framework to examine whether a controller could influence another language model's emotional trajectory.

  • Applied hidden-state activation steering within selected transformer layers.
  • Integrated sentiment and emotion classifiers with Bayesian optimization of controller parameters.
  • Compared controlled dialogue behavior under prompt-based and representation-level interventions.

Alpha-Synuclein Detection Using Brain Organoids

KAIST · Individual Research · Prof. Minee Choi

Investigated α-synuclein aggregation using circular dichroism spectroscopy and midbrain organoid-conditioned media.
